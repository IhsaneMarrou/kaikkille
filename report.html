Project Report

About the project

Kaikkille is a fictional second-hand shop , the idea behind it as it says in the title it is for everyone, no matter what gender or age someone can be there is a piece for them in my shop .

My design choices

I wanted to make a simple and minimalist web-shop , even the colors i choosed are so neutral.

What i learned?

Basically i learned everything from scratch, i started the project with little to 0 knowledge about html , giving us this project is the best way to get familiar with coding and html honestly . Doing this website i learned more about css, Flexbox, grid , images , svg , responsive design , all i knew before was the (p) and the heading tags.

What i enjoyed?

I enjoyed being creative with the headings, ideas and the color palette, making this site i thought its difficult but actually it was fun.

Final thoughts

Thank you for giving us this project it helped me learn alot about html and how it interact with css , so now it is not scary for me anymore , and everytime i am in a webpage i analyze how it was made and most of the time i understand the structure behind it , so it is not confusing as it was couple weeks ago before i start this project.
